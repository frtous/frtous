## Francesc Rodriguez Tous

Senior Lecturer in Banking at Bayes Business School, City St George's, University of London,
where I direct the MSc in Banking and International Finance.

My research is on banking regulation, macroprudential policy, monetary policy transmission,
and mortgage and household credit markets.
